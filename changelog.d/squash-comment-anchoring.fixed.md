bump: minor

- **The squash-message tool no longer deletes or replaces other comments, and a slightly long squash message posts with a warning instead of failing.** `forge-pr-squash-comment` now treats only a comment that *starts* with its hidden tag as a squash comment, so a wrap-up that quotes the squash message is never deleted as an old squash comment or re-posted in its place; `forge-pr-wrapup validate` refuses a wrap-up that embeds a squash-message section. The 50-word squash cap becomes a target: 51–55 words posts with a warning showing what to trim (also under `--dry-run`), and over 55 is still refused with the "cut N" breakdown — so an agent no longer loops re-sending a message one or two words over. The tool's documented output example now matches what it prints.
