- **Tag-per-merge auto-tagging** — `forge-changelog auto-tag` (wired into the tag-release workflow) reads the last tag, takes the strongest semver level among the fragments merged since it (tag-tree membership marks consumption), bumps, and pushes the annotated tag — no commit to the base branch, no silent path: with `[tool.forge.release].auto = "merge"` every fragment-carrying merge is tagged the moment its CI passes; otherwise the job emits a loud pending-fragments warning. The version guard now accepts a manifest at or behind the latest tag in fragments mode (the manifest syncs at assembly PRs).
