bump: patch
- A lint or formatting problem that the tooling can repair itself no longer passes a continuous-integration run. The step repaired what it could and reported that as a pass, which is right on a workstation — the repair goes into the commit being made — but wrong on a runner, where nothing is committed, so the repair was computed, thrown away, and the run went green. Every repairable problem, formatting included, was therefore unable to fail a run. On a runner the step now reports instead of repairing. Nothing changes on a workstation.
- A lint suppression that suppresses nothing now fails instead of being removed silently. One such suppression was added, removed, re-added and removed again across four pull requests, every one of them passing, because removing it counted as a repair. Seven that had never suppressed anything are gone.
